# Prompts for the campaign-planning thread

Use these in the thread that holds the real campaign, to gather canon. Paste the answers into this repo (or to an agent) as markdown.

## 1. Ariadne and the missing past (answered; see `canon/ariadne-and-backstory.md`)

Note: this prompt assumed a reunion with Ariadne. The planner correctly pointed out she has been aboard since Session 0, so prompt 3 corrects it.

```
I'm designing a mobile narrative game using my campaign as source material and a theme guide (not a literal
adaptation). I need facts about Ariadne and the surrounding backstory. Please write rough notes in one markdown
code block, mark anything uncertain as (TBD) rather than guessing.

1. Ariadne: who she is, what she was to Marcus Torin, where and when they last met, and what she knows.
   What does she want now? Is she hunted, hiding, or searching for him?
2. Marcus's missing memories: which people and events are lost, and which fragments does he half-know?
   List each as a piece of EVIDENCE the world could show (a recording, a ship's transponder, a name on a
   manifest, someone who recognises him), never as his own thoughts. Marcus is never written for.
3. The pursuers: who they are, what they want from him, how they find him, and what they would do if they caught up.
4. Other people from that time: names, roles, and whether each is an ally, a threat, or unknown.
5. The truth: why were the memories lost, and what is the answer to the sealed folder, the cut continuity mast
   and the number that matches no chart?
6. Fixed points: events from the campaign that must be true, and what is free to change.
7. Tone: what should the player feel when they finally reach Ariadne? (relief, dread, guilt, something else.)

Constraints: teen and up; mature themes without gore; no sexual content; no reproduced tabletop rulebook text or
trademarked setting terms.
```

## 2. Season planning (after the answers to prompt 1)

```
I'm turning my campaign into a mobile narrative game and need a rough "Season 1" planning document.
Please write it as one markdown document in a code block, using these sections. Be concrete, rough is fine,
and mark anything uncertain as (TBD) rather than guessing.

1. Season premise: one paragraph, plus the central question the season asks the player.
2. The mystery: what is actually going on (sealed folder, cut continuity mast, the number that matches no
   chart, Marcus's memory fragment). Give the true answer, then what is revealed when, and what is a red herring.
3. Fixed points: events that must happen from the real campaign, and what is free to change.
4. Factions and key NPCs: for each, what they want, what they'd do to get it, and one line of voice.
5. Locations: name, role in the story, tone, and one memorable image each.
6. Episode list (6-10): for each, give: title, location, the problem, 2-3 ways to approach it,
   what it costs (Credits, Strain with the Compact, crew trust, time), and what it reveals or sets up.
7. Recurring choices with long-term consequences (things the player decided earlier that come back later).
8. Endings: how many, and what separates them.
9. Content lines: tone and anything off-limits (teen and up; mature themes without gore; no sexual content).

Constraints: Marcus Torin is never written for (the game never decides his words, thoughts or actions).
No reproduced tabletop rulebook text or trademarked setting terms.
```

## 3. Follow-ups after the first answers (see `canon/design-impact.md` section 6)

```
Thank you. Your notes corrected our premise: Ariadne is already aboard, not someone to find. Follow-ups, short
answers are fine, mark anything uncertain as (TBD):

1. What does Ariadne want now? Give 5 to 8 sample lines in her voice (short; spoilers are fine, I will filter).
   How does she speak to Marcus versus to Miri?
2. Confirm or correct our crew roles: Tally, Cass, Miri, Wren, Lysa. Which are Lantern Six, which are not,
   and roughly how old is each? (We have Tally as a young pilot; your notes say eldest and anchor.)
3. Which fixed points are backstory only (shown as evidence), and which should be seen on screen in a
   30-minute game for new players?
4. Which of these GM-ONLY items may the game use? (yes/no each)
   - Ariadne tells Marcus things only if asked
   - opening an authenticated channel for Ariadne tells the Section where she is
   - the Section as the pursuers, with its "custody as protection" argument
   - Ariadne holds a removal record of what Marcus asked to remove
   - the Section holds an image of Ariadne "before Marcus"
   - Merrow as the man in both doorways
5. Should coherence and Lantern powers be visible in the game, or stay unexplained strangeness?
   (Our original brief said no psychics on screen.)
6. Child-welfare treatment: we plan records, evidence and aftermath only, with no on-screen harm to children. OK?
7. The number: which ship or Quiet Registry segment does it refer to? A placeholder is fine.
8. Is "relief mixed with guilt" the right ending tone, and is "who gets to decide what a person keeps, and what
   will you pay to protect that right?" the right season question?
```
