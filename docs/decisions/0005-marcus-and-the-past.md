# 0005. Marcus is never written for; the past is evidence

Status: accepted

## Context
Marcus Torin is the player. His missing memories and the people pursuing him are central, but the game must never decide his speech, thoughts or actions.

## Decision
- Prose narrates the world and NPCs only. Choices are the player's intent.
- Memories surface as evidence the world shows: a recording, a manifest, a transponder history, a stranger who knows his face. Never as his interior thoughts.
- Ariadne is the anchor of the story. Nothing about her is canon until the owner supplies it (`docs/planner-prompts.md`).

## Consequences
- Writers and content agents have a clear, checkable rule (it is in the PR checklist).
- Placeholder facts about the past must be marked as placeholders.
