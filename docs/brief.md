# Game Brief (received from the campaign planner thread)

Summary of the key points used for the prototype (full original text retained in chat history):

- **Setting:** dark space opera, frontier held together by charters/berth rights/arms registers (Compact at Hearthline ring-station). Exploration + mystery first; doing right costs something. Credits tight; hull upkeep.
- **Protagonist:** Marcus Torin, captain of a small independent alliance (the "Lighthouse"). NEVER written for: the game must not decide his speech, thoughts or actions. Main ship *Errant Pawn*; fleet of four hulls.
- **Sample scene:** "The Written Clause" at the Compact's hall. NPCs: Renata (chair), Priya (seneschal/liaison), Halvorsen (officer). Three approaches: press for parity (Persuade/Diplomat), ask for protected-escort status (Leadership/Tactics), take the pages away for Priya to read (no roll).
- **Mechanics:** 2d6 + skill + characteristic modifier vs 8+, hidden behind one "Try it" button; three outcomes (Clean success / Success with a cost / Failure that moves the story); target shown only on "Odds". Six characteristics 2-12 (mod about -2..+2, 10 = +1). Skills 0-4. Edge pool of 3 per episode (re-roll a failure or upgrade a cost-success to clean). Resources: Credits (reserve ~Cr40,000), upkeep/fuel, crew standing, Strain with the Compact, Edge.
- **Visuals:** deep navy ground, amber structure, green status; dark + light themes; clean minimal, faint instrument-panel feel (small caps labels, monospaced numbers); no CRT scanlines. Later: slow parallax, jump shimmer.
- **Content:** teen-and-up, mature themes without gore, no sexual content. Adaptation of a solo tabletop campaign; do not reproduce published rulebook text or trademarked setting.
