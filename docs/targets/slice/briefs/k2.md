# K2 The Silent Hab (keystone brief)

Status: draft brief for the writer (a strong model, then the owner). Planner answers filed in `docs/canon/keystones/k2.md` (PR #122; this PR is stacked on it). Read `docs/SLICE.md` and `docs/CONTENT_GENERATION.md` first.

> **Design packet, not assembler input.** `npm run build:prompt` and `npm run assemble:scene` take one three-approach roll only; this keystone has more structure and is written by hand with a strong model. Facts the planner has not confirmed stay `[STUB]`.

- **Archetype:** distress call, elevated, with the slice's ship-versus-ship opening. **When:** cycle 2 or 3 (forced by the cycle 3 deadline).
- **Location:** `docs/canon/locations/traffic-hab.md`. **Backdrops:** `skirmish` for beat 1, `hab` for beat 2.
- **Length:** about 350 words read, about 1,400 authored with outcomes (two rolls).

## Premise

A hab on a dark lane is failing, and nothing answers hails. The hab has a harbourmaster, a ledger, and recordings it sold to pay for air seals (all played). Before your crew can dock, an **unmarked cutter** has come to strip it. Beat 1 is the lane. Beat 2 is the rescue.

**Words to avoid:** "hijack crew" (the planner has no played term; "raiders" is played and may be used if a word is needed, but "wreckers" is tied to the forgery thread; otherwise leave the cutter unnamed and unmarked), "Lantern" or "Lanterns", and anything that explains the manifest. A counterfeit distress call is a played pattern; use it only as an optional twist, never as the explanation.

## Beat 1: the intercept (ship versus ship)

Three approaches, nine outcomes. Your ship's part shows in the Odds panel once issue #91 lands: `armed` helps the second approach, `fast` the first.

| Approach label | Skills | Spirit |
|---|---|---|
| Run the cutter down | Pilot, Astrogation | speed and nerve; the cutter breaks off or turns on you |
| Open fire to drive it off | Gunnery, Tactics | a short exchange; hits, near misses, the hab hearing it |
| Jam its sensors and talk it off | Electronics, Diplomat | a bargain over open channel; the hab hears the offer |

Clean: the cutter breaks off and you arrive with time in hand. Cost: it breaks off but your ship or the hab pays. Fail: the cutter docks first and the rescue starts under pressure. Failure moves the story on. Ramming and gunfire are within the content line. No named hulls, no named people.

| Approach | Clean | Cost | Fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| Run it down | Credits +1,500 | Credits -1,000 | Credits -500 |
| Open fire | Renown +1 | Credits -1,500 | Credits -500 |
| Jam and talk | Credits +1,000 | Credits -500 | Credits -1,500 |

Pursuit: Open fire is the loud choice (Pursuit +1, per `game-design.md` section 3); the other two are quiet. Listed here so the cost is said before it is paid; the owner confirms this reading. Strain is not used here: the Compact has no desk on this lane.

## Beat 2: the rescue (people or records)

The hab cannot be saved whole. Your crew can carry out the people or pull the records, and time and air allow one properly.

- **The people.** About 109 residents, all adult, never seen on screen. Show the harbourmaster, a voice at the counter, a lane sealed, a lock cycling. Never describe suffering, never any child. Do not state a count of the lost.
- **The records.** The ledger and a manifest of **six bare identifiers, one matching no one** (a played detail). **Do not say who the unmatched identifier is. Do not say "Lanterns" or "children".** Show the manifest as a printed block of six bare strings (invented `[STUB]` identifiers, never real ones) and the unmatched one marked only by "no match on file".

| Approach label | Skills | Spirit |
|---|---|---|
| Seal the lanes and ferry the residents out | Leadership, Pilot | people first; records are lost or damaged |
| Pull the ledger and manifest core | Electronics, Mechanic | records first; the people wait on the harbourmaster |
| Split the crew and do both badly | Tactics, Persuade | both partial; costs both ways |

| Approach | Clean | Cost | Fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seal and ferry | Renown +1 | Credits -1,000 | Credits -500 |
| Pull the core | Credits +1,500 | Credits -1,500 | Credits -500 |
| Split | Credits +1,000 | Credits -1,000 | Credits -1,500 |

## The piece it yields (`six`)

If the records are pulled (the second approach clean or cost, or the third clean), the player holds the **full manifest**. Otherwise the player holds a **fragment**: a header line and the count of six. Both count for K5, which needs K2 and K3 only in the slice.

## Evidence the world shows

The manifest block. The harbourmaster's switch ("the way you would close a door you meant to stay shut", played narration in S4E2, so the sentence itself is playable; the harbourmaster is "she"). Nothing about Marcus. No speech, thought or action for him.

## Flags (budget: two)

- `k2_records_saved`: true when the full manifest is held.
- `k2_people_first`: true when the people were taken out first (K5 may mention the hab by one flat registry line).

## Places

the hab traffic counter, the harbourmaster's switch, the docking arm, the council. Nothing else named.

## Do not

Name or explain the cutter's crew; name or explain the manifest; write for Marcus; show any child; use `[GM-ONLY]` or HELD facts; use game words in prose.
