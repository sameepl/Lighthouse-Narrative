# K1 The Passenger (keystone brief)

Status: draft brief for the writer (a strong model, then the owner). Planner answers filed in `docs/canon/keystones/k1.md` (PR #122; this PR is stacked on it). Placeholder facts stay `[STUB]`. Read `docs/SLICE.md` and `docs/CONTENT_GENERATION.md` first.

> **Design packet, not assembler input.** `npm run build:prompt` and `npm run assemble:scene` take one three-approach roll only; this keystone has more structure and is written by hand with a strong model. Facts the planner has not confirmed stay `[STUB]`.

- **Archetype:** courier, elevated. **Layer:** keystone. **When:** cycle 1, second action (forced by its deadline).
- **Location:** `docs/canon/locations/hearthline-berths.md` (the long cold bay, the gate, the berth office), at the thin hour.
- **Length:** about 350 words read, about 1,000 authored with outcomes. **Backdrop:** `berths`.

## Premise

A routine courier booking at the berths: carry one passenger out to a hab on the lane. The passenger is hunted. Two pressures close in, both public and unnamed:

1. a **creditor's collection writ** held by a clerk at the berth office, and
2. a **fast unmarked hull** that scans the berth once and leaves.

The Compact is watching the gate, so openness has a price. Nothing else is known about the passenger or why they are hunted.

## The passenger (a role, never a name)

Pick **one** role and stay in it: a transfer clerk or a salvage appraiser (the planner also offered a relief hand off a failed hab; it is not used, so K1 does not link to K2's hab). Adult. Not a former crewmate from an old ship (that would be Contact 1, HELD). Never named. Never gives a reason for the pursuit beyond the writ and the hull.

## The evidence the world shows

At the berth office terminal, when the booking is read, the screen prints an **access-log line** against a pass on the ship's register (the planner: do not show the holder): "Survey Service personnel authorisation, not revoked, one prior access." Show only the line and the passenger's reaction to it: they look at the screen, then at your crew, and say nothing. Do not show the holder, an age, or anything about childhood. Do not say how it connects to the past. Marcus gets no speech and no thoughts; the line sits on the screen.

## The hard choice (three approaches, nine outcomes)

| Approach label | Skills | Quiet or loud | Spirit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shelter them aboard until the writ lapses | Electronics, Persuade | quiet | hide them; a crew member bluffs the writ clerk; keep the Compact content |
| Hand them to the writ clerk | Persuade, Diplomat | quiet | pay or negotiate; the passenger is lost to the writ |
| Smuggle them out past the gate | Pilot, Tactics | loud | fast and visible; the unmarked hull will remember |

Each has clean, cost and fail outcomes per the contract. Failure moves the story on: the passenger still reaches a named route (a relief hab on the lane, the registry counter, the gate officer's desk). A crew member is always the actor; Marcus acts and speaks nowhere.

## Effects (placeholders, inside the bands)

| Approach | Clean | Cost | Fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shelter | Strain -1 | Credits -1,000 | Strain +1 |
| Hand over | Credits +1,500 | Credits +500, Strain +1 | Credits -500 |
| Smuggle | Renown +1 | Credits -500 | Strain +1 |

Pursuit: Smuggle is the loud choice. Pursuit +1 applies once, on **any** outcome of Smuggle (clean, cost or fail), per `game-design.md` section 3 and `WRITING.md`. Shelter and Hand over are quiet, and **quiet means no Pursuit change in the slice** (the contract's -1 for a quiet choice is not used here; owner to confirm). Listed here so the cost is said before it is paid; the owner confirms this reading. Strain is the Compact's patience, and the Compact is watching the gate, so Strain fits K1. Every effect lands on your crew's side; give each an `effect_reason` that names the other party.

## Flags and hooks (budget: none)

K1 sets **no flags** in the slice. The access-log line is shown unconditionally, so a flag would be redundant, and nothing downstream reads whether the passenger was kept. Its effects are the Pursuit tick and the ledger.

## Places the writer may use

the berth office, the gate, the gate officer's desk, the registry counter, the writ clerk's desk, a relief hab on the lane. Nothing else named.

## Do not

- Name the passenger or the pursuers. Write "Section", "Office" or "Hunter". Use any `[GM-ONLY]` or HELD fact.
- Write any speech, thought or action for Marcus.
- Show harm to a child, or a child.
- Use game words in prose (strain, renown, effect, roll).
- Add a fact about the passenger or the pursuers beyond the writ, the unmarked hull and the log line.
