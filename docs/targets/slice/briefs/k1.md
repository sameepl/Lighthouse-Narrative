- **Archetype:** courier, elevated. **Layer:** keystone. **When:** cycle 1, second action (forced by its deadline).
- **Location:** `docs/canon/locations/hearthline-berths.md` (the long cold bay, the gate, the berth office), at the thin hour.
- **Length:** about 350 words read, about 1,000 authored with outcomes. **Backdrop:** `berths`.

## Premise

A routine courier booking at the berths: carry one passenger out to a hab on the lane. The passenger is hunted. Two pressures close in, both public and unnamed:

1. a **creditor's collection writ** held by a clerk at the berth office, and
2. a **fast unmarked hull** that scans the berth once and leaves.

The Compact is watching the gate, so openness has a price. The passenger has been told a lot about your crew that no stranger should know, but never says how.

## The passenger (a role, never a name)

Pick **one** role and stay in it: a transfer clerk, a salvage appraiser, or a relief hand off a failed hab. Adult. Not a former crewmate from an old ship (that would be Contact 1, HELD). Never named. Never gives a reason for the pursuit beyond the writ and the hull.

## The evidence the world shows

At the berth office terminal, when the booking is read, the screen prints an **access-log line** against your crew's pass: "Survey Service personnel authorisation, not revoked, one prior access." Show only the line and the passenger's reaction to it: they look at the screen, then at your crew, and say nothing. Do not show the holder, an age, or anything about childhood. Do not say how it connects to the past. Marcus gets no speech and no thoughts; the line sits on the screen.

## The hard choice (three approaches, nine outcomes)

| Approach label | Skills | Quiet or loud | Spirit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shelter them aboard until the writ lapses | Electronics, Persuade | quiet | hide, bluff the clerk, keep the Compact content |
| Hand them to the writ clerk | Persuade, Diplomat | quiet | pay or negotiate; the passenger is lost to the writ |
| Smuggle them out past the gate | Pilot, Tactics | loud | fast and visible; the unmarked hull will remember |

Each has clean, cost and fail outcomes per the contract. Failure moves the story on: the passenger still reaches a named route (a relief hab, a registry counter, the gate officer's desk).

## Effects (placeholders, inside the bands)

| Approach | Clean | Cost | Fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shelter | Strain -1 | Credits -1,000 | Strain +1 |
| Hand over | Credits +1,500 | Credits +500, Strain +1 | Credits -500 |
| Smuggle | Renown +1 | Credits -500 | Strain +1 |

Pursuit: Smuggle is the loud choice (Pursuit +1 once, per `game-design.md` section 3 and `WRITING.md`); Shelter and Hand over are quiet. Listed here so the cost is said before it is paid; the owner confirms this reading. Strain is the Compact's patience, and the Compact is watching the gate, so Strain fits K1. Every effect lands on your crew's side; give each an `effect_reason` that names the other party.

## Flags and hooks (budget: two flags)

- `k1_log_seen`: true once the access-log line has been shown (K5 may refer back to it as evidence).

## Places the writer may use

the berth office, the gate, the writ clerk's desk, a relief hab on the lane. Nothing else named.

## Do not

- Name the passenger or the pursuers. Write "Section", "Office" or "Hunter". Use any `[GM-ONLY]` or HELD fact.
- Write any speech, thought or action for Marcus.
- Show harm to a child, or a child.
- Use game words in prose (strain, renown, effect, roll).
