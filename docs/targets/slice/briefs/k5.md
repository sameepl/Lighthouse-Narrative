> **Design packet, not assembler input.** `npm run build:prompt` and `npm run assemble:scene` take one three-approach roll only; this keystone has more structure and is written by hand with a strong model. Facts the planner has not confirmed stay `[STUB]`.

- **Archetype:** finale. **When:** cycle 4, forced on its schedule (issue #113), whatever the Pursuit clock says.
- **Location:** inside the ship, `docs/canon/ships/errant-pawn.md`. **Backdrop:** `pawn`; the endings use `end_open` and `end_closed`.

## Premise

The pursuit has closed in. In the quiet of the ship, Ariadne gives a plain "I am not able to say" (her played line separates it from "I do not know", S4E1). Then she asks whether an old request still stands. The player answers by acts on the console, never by speech.

## Ariadne's lines (follow `canon/ariadne-voice.md`)

- She informs and never instructs. She says which kind of "no" it is, and for the removal record it is **"I am not able to say"** (never "I do not know").
- The request on screen reads exactly: **"Request on record: remove memories, to protect the others."** Omit "child", the night, any adult in a doorway, and what was removed.
- The image: one flat registry line, **"an earlier image of her, held elsewhere"**. Do not write "childless". The player never decides what happens to that image: it is her call, and she says so.
- She never explains the discontinuities, and never shows anything as a power.

## Which request

The request is **the removal request**: "Request on record: remove memories, to protect the others." (The earlier design had her asking whether he still wants to be told the truth. The planner's answer replaces that, and the `[NEW]` sample line about being told the truth is **not used** in K5.) Reaffirm keeps the request on record. Withdraw strikes it. Ariadne never says what was removed.

## What shapes the scene

- **Pieces are engine state** (`six`, `removed`), not flags. K2 always grants `six` (full manifest, or a fragment); K3 always grants `removed`. Neither is ever missing in the slice, since both keystones are forced. K5 adds one evidence line per piece on the console: `removed`, one flat record line; `six`, a full line if the manifest is held (`k2_records_saved`), otherwise a fragment line (a header and the count of six). K4 and `claim` are not in the slice.
- **A full clock** (`pursuitFull`, issue #113): the closing roll is one step harder (target +1) and the harder closing lines are used. **Both choices stay available**; it never removes an ending and never triggers the scene early.
- **Flags read:** `k2_people_first` and `k3_published` may each add one flat line (the hab's registry line; a filed chart note). Nothing more.

## The decision (two endings, a visible cost each)

| Choice | Act on the console | Ending |
|---|---|---|
| Reaffirm the request | confirm the record stays on file and sealed | `end_closed` |
| Withdraw the request | strike the record from the file | `end_open` |

(Mapping corrected 2026-11-04: the request is to remove memories, so keeping it closes the door and striking it opens one. The owner confirms.)

The decision itself is a choice (no roll). A roll then sets how cleanly it lands, with three closing variants per ending (clean, cost, fail) and a ledger line each:

| Ending | Roll (skills) | Clean | Cost | Fail |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reaffirm | Electronics, Mechanic | Credits +1,000 | Credits -500 | Credits -1,000 |
| Withdraw | Electronics, Recon | Credits +1,500 | Credits -1,000 | Credits -500 |

Console skills only: Marcus is not made to persuade or lead anyone. A **fail** here has no route to offer, since the run is ending: it lands with the harder closing lines and its ledger cost, and the ledger line names what the crew carries forward.

**Cost is a ledger line and the ending, not Pursuit.** Strain is not used: the Compact has no part in this scene. No victory framing. Each ending carries a visible cost, **chosen only from these nouns**: a record, a sealed line, time, trust, credits, a berth. Never name what was removed. Reaffirm: the cost is what stays sealed (a sealed line, a record kept). Withdraw: the cost is what is let go (a record struck, trust). Relief mixed with guilt (the planner's tone) is carried by Ariadne's lines and the ledger, never as Marcus's feeling. The foreclosure ending (#29) is a separate path and does not run through K5.
