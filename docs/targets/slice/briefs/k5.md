# K5 The Asking (keystone brief)

Status: draft brief for the writer (the strongest model, then the owner). Planner answers filed in `docs/canon/keystones/k5.md` and `across-all.md` (PR #122; this PR is stacked on it). Read `docs/SLICE.md`, `docs/canon/ariadne-voice.md` and `docs/CONTENT_GENERATION.md` first.

> **Design packet, not assembler input.** `npm run build:prompt` and `npm run assemble:scene` take one three-approach roll only; this keystone has more structure and is written by hand with a strong model. Facts the planner has not confirmed stay `[STUB]`.

- **Archetype:** finale. **When:** cycle 4, forced on its schedule (issue #113), whatever the Pursuit clock says.
- **Location:** inside the ship, `docs/canon/ships/errant-pawn.md`. **Backdrop:** `pawn`; the endings use `end_open` and `end_closed`.
- **Length:** about 350 words read, about 1,200 authored.

## Premise

The pursuit has closed in. In the quiet of the ship, Ariadne gives her first honest "I am not able to say". Then she asks whether an old request still stands. The player answers by acts on the console, never by speech.

## Ariadne's lines (follow `canon/ariadne-voice.md`)

- She informs and never instructs. She says which kind of "no" it is, and for the removal record it is **"I am not able to say"** (never "I do not know").
- The request on screen reads exactly: **"Request on record: remove memories, to protect the others."** Omit "child", the night, any adult in a doorway, and what was removed.
- The image: one flat registry line, **"an earlier image of her, held elsewhere"**. Do not write "childless". The player never decides what happens to that image: it is her call, and she says so.
- She never explains the discontinuities, and never shows anything as a power.

## What shapes the scene

- **Pieces held:** `six` (K2, full manifest or fragment) and `removed` (K3). Each adds one evidence line to the console, shown as a record. K4 and `claim` are not in the slice.
- **A full clock:** if the Pursuit clock filled early (`pursuitFull`, issue #113), the scene is harder: fewer options, a shorter hour, and the harder closing lines. It does not trigger the scene early.
- **K2 and K3 flags** may add one flat line each (for example the hab's registry line). Nothing more.

## The decision (two endings, a visible cost each)

| Choice | Act on the console | Ending |
|---|---|---|
| Reaffirm the request | confirm the record is kept and sealed again | `end_open` |
| Withdraw the request | strike the record and keep what remains | `end_closed` |

The decision itself is a choice (no roll). A roll then sets how cleanly it lands, with three closing variants per ending (clean, cost, fail) and a ledger line each:

| Ending | Roll (skills) | Clean | Cost | Fail |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reaffirm | Electronics, Persuade | Credits +1,000 | Credits -500 | Credits -1,000 |
| Withdraw | Leadership, Tactics | Renown +1 | Credits -1,000 | Credits -500 |

**Cost is a ledger line and the ending, not Pursuit.** Strain is not used: the Compact has no part in this scene. No victory framing. Each ending carries a visible cost: the reaffirm ending names what is still kept in the dark; the withdraw ending names what is let go. Relief mixed with guilt (the planner's tone). The foreclosure ending (#29) is a separate path and does not run through K5.

## Flags (budget: one)

- `k5_request_kept`: true on reaffirm. It selects the ending and the ledger summary.

## Places

the console, the viewport, the registry line. Nothing else named.

## Do not
