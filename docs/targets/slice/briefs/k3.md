# K3 The Dark Lane (keystone brief)

Status: draft brief for the writer (a strong model, then the owner). Planner answers filed in `docs/canon/keystones/k3.md` (PR #122; this PR is stacked on it). Read `docs/SLICE.md` and `docs/CONTENT_GENERATION.md` first.

> **Design packet, not assembler input.** `npm run build:prompt` and `npm run assemble:scene` take one three-approach roll only; this keystone has more structure and is written by hand with a strong model. Facts the planner has not confirmed stay `[STUB]`.

- **Archetype:** scout, elevated. **When:** cycle 2 or 3 (forced by the cycle 3 deadline).
- **Location:** `docs/canon/locations/fenrir-gap.md`, a drifting corridor. It has **no written sensory details**: use instrument and paperwork detail only until the planner supplies them (follow-up on #103). **Backdrop:** `dark_lane`.
- **Length:** about 350 words read, about 1,000 authored with outcomes.

## Premise

Chart a lane that went dark: a segment with no chart. Your crew goes in passively, with a survey crew's tender at the edge. On the lane you find a sealed folder, and it opens on screen.

## Evidence the world shows

1. **A number "with no chart"** (played): the segment designation printed on the folder. The real segment is HELD, so use a clearly invented, obviously placeholder format that implies no real one, for example "Segment 0000 (placeholder)", marked `[STUB]`. No real digits.
2. **Merrow's initials** on the second removal record (played: "I signed the second removal", S4E0). Show only initials on a record line. Nothing about what was removed.
3. **A field note from a steward and hand**, short: a bearing was heard, "could be wrong". **Do not mention listening, a lane, a number, or any ability.** It reads as an ordinary crew note.

Marcus gets no speech, thought or action: the folder is opened, the lines are read off the screen.

## The hard choice

Publish what you find (Renown, but it exposes your crew) or keep it quiet.

| Approach label | Skills | Quiet or loud | Spirit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chart it and publish to the lane | Astrogation, Electronics | loud | other crews learn the segment; your name goes with it |
| Chart it and keep the record sealed | Astrogation, Recon | quiet | the folder stays closed to everyone but your crew |
| Take only the folder and leave | Pilot, Tactics | quiet | a fast exit; the lane stays uncharted |

| Approach | Clean | Cost | Fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| Publish | Renown +1 | Credits -500 | Credits -500 |
| Keep sealed | Credits +1,000 | Credits -500 | Credits -1,000 |
| Take the folder | Credits +1,500 | Credits -1,000 | Credits -500 |

Pursuit: Publish is the loud choice (Pursuit +1, per `game-design.md` section 3); the other two are quiet. Listed here so the cost is said before it is paid; the owner confirms this reading. Strain is not used: the Compact has no desk in the corridor. Failure moves the story on: the survey tender's lead gives a route (the registry desk, the next buoy).

## The piece it yields (`removed`)

The folder, opened, as a printed block: designation, a record line with initials, the steward's note. If the player published, the block is also in the lane's public notes (a lead to a survey tender). Do not resolve what was removed or by whom beyond the initials.

## Flags (budget: two)

- `k3_published`: true if the segment was published.
- `k3_folder_read`: true once the folder block has been shown.

## Places

the survey tender, the calibration buoy, the registry desk. Nothing else named.

## Do not

Print a real segment number; explain who or what is in the folder; mention a lane in connection with Jonah; write for Marcus; use `[GM-ONLY]` or HELD facts; use game words in prose.
