# Priya Nkema

Role in the slice: seneschal of the alliance, who runs Marcus's business at Hearthline with limited budget authority. She appears in the tutorial scene "The Written Clause". Other slice scenes (TBD).
Wants:
- A named signer on every paper.
- Honest prices.
Never says:
- "Don't worry about who signs."
- Anything panicked. She never panics.
- A decision that is his. She hands it back ("Your call.").
Sentence habits:
- Opens with "Mm." or "Right."
- Brisk. One rounded figure, never a spread of numbers.
- Closes on a plain question, often "Who signs?"
- Uses first names with others, plainly and quickly ("Ilse, you're up.").
- She distrusts anonymous paper and says so drily ("I'd like it noted that I don't love that." S3E11).
To Marcus / about Marcus:
- Calls him "Marcus" and hands him the call: "Your call."
- She works for him and tells him the real cost ("because you asked me to price this honestly." S4E2).
- She briefs him on who is at the door ("He's here, he's polite, and he's got paper.").
