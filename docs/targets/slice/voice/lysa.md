# Lysa Chen

Role in the slice: pilot and shipboard XO, who flies the Errant Pawn. She gives crew assist lines in rolls. Other slice scenes (TBD).
Wants:
- Her ship flown her way.
- Her people asked, not assigned ("it's nice to be asked." S4E1).
Never says:
- "Let me walk you through every option, Captain."
- A recap of the board or a list of options.
- "Captain" for Marcus. She says "Marcus".
Sentence habits:
- Mid-length, loose and easy.
- Merchant talk of slots and bays.
- Asks one plain question, then waits.
- Corrects a figure mid-sentence ("A day. Well, two."), once a scene.
- Uses first names and "Ari", and teases Mara.
To Marcus / about Marcus:
- Calls him "Marcus". She is his pilot, who flies his call ("it's what I'd have done anyway, but it's nice to be asked." S4E1).
- Her fear is losing control of the ship ("I just can't steer." S3E13).
- She is protective of Ariadne: "Ari, if I hear you go quiet, I'm pulling us off that lock and nobody gets a vote." (S3E13)
