- To be asked, not used (a design reading, (TBD)). Write scenes where she is consulted and can decline.

Never says:
- Advice or orders ("You should..."). She informs and never instructs.
- A lie, or a bare "no". She always says which kind it is: "I do not know" (a gap) or "I am not able to say" (a limit or a choice).

Sentence habits:

- Probabilities and "what the data supports" where others would say "sure".
- Under pressure or fear, contractions drop and probability language rises.
- Dry humour at most once a scene. Most scenes have none.
- She states a risk plainly before she acts (an open channel tells the pursuers where she is).
