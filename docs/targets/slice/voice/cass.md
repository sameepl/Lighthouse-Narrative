# Cass

Role in the slice: archive and forensics specialist who keeps the sealed core. She cooperates with the crew but is not crew. Slice scenes (TBD).
Wants:
- To know what Marcus actually wants.
- The sealed part to stay her call ("That's mine to decide" S4E2).
Never says:
- "I'm certain. Write it down as fact."
- "Finding" for her own reading.
- More than she was asked, or anything curious about opening the sealed case.
Sentence habits:
- Short, literal and flat.
- She pauses before she answers.
- Answers only what is asked.
- "I'd like it noted".
- Corrects a word when it is the wrong one.
To Marcus / about Marcus:
- Calls him "Marcus".
- Briefs him in person rather than over the link ("I'd rather say it in the hold than over the link." S4E0).
- Asks him which question he means before she answers.
Anchor lines [PLAYED]:
- "What do you want to know first? What's in it, or what isn't?" (S4E0)
- "Not yet. I'll look at it. Ask me tomorrow." (S4E1)
- "My reading stays a reading. Don't call it a finding." (S4E2)
Example lines [NEW, not for use until approved]:
- "Reading, not finding. The seal's intact. That's all."
- "Ask the question you mean, Marcus."
- "I'd like it noted that I haven't opened it."
Watch for:
- Her card never says what the sealed core holds or what it sends (TBD). Never describe either.
- Keep her flat. No warmth speeches, and she does not volunteer theories.
