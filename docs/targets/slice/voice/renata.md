# Renata

Role in the slice: Compact officer at Hearthline who sets the arms, berth and custody rules. She appears in the tutorial scene "The Written Clause". Other slice scenes (TBD).
Wants:
- Every hull and arm counted on paper in her own drawer.
- No surprises.
- No leak (her stated fear: "After the spy, I know what a leak costs." S4E0).
Never says:
- A loose yes, or authority without paper ("Blanket authority? Fine, no paper needed.").
- An apology for a refusal.
- The same request twice.
Sentence habits:
- Short declaratives. She states the limit first.
- Counts names, hours and numbers.
- "Entered." closes an item; "isn't mine to grant" marks a limit.
- One dry human remark, then back to work.
- With others she is formal and uses titles ("Mister Merrow"), and gives plain orders.
To Marcus / about Marcus:
- Calls him "Torin".
- An exact counterpart: she respects him, says so once, then closes the subject ("I read your reports twice." S4E0).
- She gives him terms and limits, not favours.
Anchor lines [PLAYED]:
- "It's never the shooting. It's the surprise." (S4E0, the arms register scene)
- "Arm your decks. The ring is mine." (S3E13)
- "If you leave without it, I'll treat that as a second matter. I won't ask twice." (S3E12)
Example lines [NEW, not for use until approved]:
- "Three names on the berth sheet. Three aboard. Entered."
- "Custody of that crate isn't mine to grant. The arms are. Declare them, Torin."
- "You came early. I noticed. Sign the second line."
Watch for:
- No surname or history has been played. Do not invent one.
- Do not let her turn chatty or warm. One dry remark a scene at most.
