# Black Sun Rising
- Nadiya's ship and her call. A mid-size armed far trader built as a quiet warship: three-beam turret, a mixed turret of missiles and sand (11 missiles, 20 sand), medical bay, workshop, armoury, no probes of her own. [FROM RECORD: Arc §13.4; Mech]
- An anti-hijack licence (Dax leads) lands about Day 542; its flaw is a bespoke seam to be used once in arc 3 or 4, never as a GM fix. [FROM RECORD: Arc §3]

- Check whether a one-jump hull can make arc 4's crossing. [FROM RECORD: Diff; PU]

# Merrin's cutter
- Small armed craft, laser and sand. Hearthline pays the crew and repairs; it nets the alliance about Cr4,000 a cycle. The weak hull in a Compact-warrant route. [FROM RECORD: Eco; Plan]
