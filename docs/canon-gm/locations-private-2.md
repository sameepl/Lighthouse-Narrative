# Far Horizon
- A ship, not a place. In Season 1 a hostile hull presenting as the survey craft Resolute compromised her with forged records, trusted voices and official-looking traffic. One man, Tom Arlen, died. The Wayward Saint's ambush crippled the hostile drive; it escaped. Her salvage funded the opening ledger. [FROM RECORD: Res §1; Mech]
- Lysa, Ren and Elias carry the history. Lysa goes quiet at the S4E0 news line; never explain it for her. [FROM RECORD: Plan §3A.5]
- That hostile hull and the Season 3 craft Resolute (CRV-1) are different ships of similar or same class or programme, not the same machine. [RULED 2026-10-20: PL1] Working reading [NEW]: a Section package hull wearing CRV-1's registered name.
- The survivors' arc 1 echo points at the method (trusted voices, forged records), not at CRV-1. They may be offered, never forced, a chance to face the relevant craft: docs/canon-gm/resolute-craft-strand.md. [RULED 2026-10-20: PL4]

# Halworth Deep Relay (hex 1317)
- A decommissioned Survey relay, unstaffed 20+ years. Nothing has been shown on screen; evidence only. [FROM RECORD: Gaz; Led]
- True: a 41-hour gap in its housekeeping record; a steady power overhead too large for an empty station; two log entries per cycle where the design calls for one. [FROM RECORD: HWF §1]
- The parked buffer was copied roughly fourteen months before the S3E5 reveal, using Marcus's own never-revoked old credential, from an archive custody terminal the Section rents. [FROM RECORD: Led, played S3E5; Idx]
- The buffer holds 41 hours of room audio and a calm voice counting. [FROM RECORD: HWF §2; design, unplayed]
- Occupant or keeper inside: HELD (decide only in front of the player at the first on-site read). The manifest's unmatched identifier (journal Q11) is a separate mystery closing at S4E8. [RULED 2026-10-20: PL7]
