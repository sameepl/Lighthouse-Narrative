# Hearthline (hex 1420)
- A ring station: real port, shipyard, repair. A berthholders' board, the Compact, governs. A sealed, visible sidearm is normal carry; concealed weapons are barred. [FROM RECORD: Gaz]
- True story: the Compact's standing (berth priority, recognition of its charter, a shield from creditors) rests on honouring Coordination Office instruments. One, "continuance authority", is an undefined clause that protects Merrow and locks Adrian Vale away. Break it and Hearthline turns informal. Renata defers for that reason; she is not a villain. [FROM RECORD: Plan §2; PU for Adrian]
- Her charter offer is generous, but its clause (b) accepts Office instruments "in force" by reference, so it takes in the clause. [FROM RECORD: Arc §4; PU]
- Hub between arcs: a cycle card plus at most one played beat. Merrow is held there as a ward; every tap goes through Renata. [FROM RECORD: Plan §5]

# The Shoal (Wenond, hex 1720, is the anchor)
- Salvage belts and small habs on the Corda side; thin ports. [FROM RECORD: Gaz]
- True story: a protection racket with a voice. The traffic hab's keeper (Philippa Grisewood, private name) sells lane recordings through an extra deck output to a buoy on the hull, booked as "lane maintenance"; the money keeps her air plant in parts. Habs that pay are never hit. [FROM RECORD: E2S]
- Eleven stealth relay buoys ("lamps" in play) cover every lane into Wenond. By the S4E2 close six are recovered and five are mapped only. [FROM RECORD: E2C, played]
- "The shoal talking": looping dead-crew beacons, plus forged lines that sometimes carry a fragment of an old sample, a long-dead captain. The GM never explains it. [FROM RECORD: Plan §3A.4]
- Traffic hab outcome: shut by contract; the keeper lives; a hull is tied down at the hab for a cycle. [FROM RECORD: E2C, played]
- Rinn's salvage camp sits in a dark belt on the far side (Lorond 2020). [NEW: placement proposal in Maps]

# Faraday Basin (hex 1320)
- Garden-type basin world; a yard cooperative (Ivara Pell) and a mooring reserve (Soren Dey). A top-class yard that can certify jump-capable hulls. One jump from Hearthline (player ruling 2026-10-17). [FROM RECORD: Gaz]
- Private: Faraday holds first right on the alliance's yard work and is also its creditor; the last instalments fall about Day 542. It paid a salvage house's Cr96,000 lien tranche in return for a service warranty. [FROM RECORD: Arc §1; Led]
- A Combine house once asked Faraday to quote a "cognition cradle coupling" for an unregistered military-pattern lot; Faraday declined. This is the first telegraph of the unfinished hull. [FROM RECORD: Plan §3B.3; PU]
