# Roll targets the GM actually used
- Routine 6+, average 8+, difficult 10+. Very hard 12+, "never for Marcus alone". Yes, 8+ is the average. [FROM RECORD: Diff §7; Mech]
- By arc: arc 1 routine 8+, one 10+ roll per episode; arc 2 routine 8+, variance and misjump 10+; arc 3 8+ with minus 1 or 2 per gas-giant layer; arc 4 the crossing 10+, a prepared approach brings it to 8+. [FROM RECORD: Diff §7; PU for arcs 2-4]
- Party leads sit at plus 3 or 4, supports plus 2, Marcus plus 3 as the face and plus 1 or 2 elsewhere. Opposition: competent plus 1, specialist plus 2, exceptional plus 3. A competent NPC has skill 1, an expert 2 to 3. [FROM RECORD: Diff §1, §4]
- Chance of success at plus 2 / plus 3: 8+ is 72% / 83%; 10+ is 42% / 58%; 12+ is 17% / 28%. One Edge adds about 20 points on a 10+ roll. [FROM RECORD: Diff §1]

# Edge and pacing numbers
- Edge 3 per episode; bonus Edge only when the fiction earns it, stated (S4E2 earned one and ended 2 of 4). Enemy Edge: 0 for weak opposition (arc 1), 1 specialist, 2 for an exceptional enemy at its own climax. [FROM RECORD: Arc §9; E2C]
- Per episode: about 5 scenes, at most 2 talk-only, one Council (4 speakers, about 300 words), one Lantern scene (3 speakers), 6 speakers per scene, 2 goals, one hub beat charged to the next arc. At most 2 forged messages per episode. [FROM RECORD: Diff §5; Plan §3A.4]
- Three rolls in a row at 8+ and plus 2 pass 37% of the time, so two scenes can go wrong by chance alone: do not add punishing rolls. [FROM RECORD: Diff §6]

# How costs were chosen
- The cost attaches to the choice, never to the dice; the dice set only the quality of the result. A cost is said before it is paid. [FROM RECORD: Diff §6]
- Counters are information and choices, not caps (ruling 2026-10-18). No windfall income and no cost by fiat to close a gap. [FROM RECORD: Arc §9; Eco]
- Typical cost currencies: an income line lost for a cycle; a hull tied down; berth priority lost; a counter tick; a favour owed; standing spent; the wolf adapting (one counter retired). [FROM RECORD: Plan §8; Arc §10]
- Money pressure is opportunity, not a deficit: capital for choices. [FROM RECORD: Eco, Season 3]
- Ten "doing right costs" decision points are set across the season (Plan §8). [FROM RECORD: Plan §8; PU beyond arc 1]
