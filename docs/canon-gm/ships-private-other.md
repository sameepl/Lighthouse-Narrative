# The wolf's hull (PU)
- A raider, about 30 crew: former lane marshals. A corsair-pattern hull uprated, with a fast boarding pinnace and berths for ransomed crew. The quickest hull in the region apart from Errant Pawn. Numbers are set at prep. [FROM RECORD: Plan §3A.3; PU]
- Its stealth relay buoys carry the forged voice, so voice bearing and hull bearing disagree. Plan says 8-10 buoys; Maps says eleven (see gaps). [FROM RECORD: Plan; Maps]
- Never fights the whole fleet together. [FROM RECORD: Plan]

# The unfinished hull, CRV-3 (PU)
- A 200-ton recovery hull in a salvage house's inventory, complete but for its core cradle and electronic tuning. Finished, it would be faster and harder to find than any alliance hull; the wolf's tool is a degraded copy of its suite. It must never appear pre-damaged. [FROM RECORD: Plan §3B]

# Faithful Compass
