WHY THESE SIX [RULED, GM backstory]
Adrian Vale chose Survey dependents who survived discontinuity exposure early and perceived the drift without being fooled, each with a different habit matched to a role.
- Marcus: exposed as an infant on a relay tender; no fixed habit, most plastic; holds the whole. EID-0 was built for him.
- Tally: a child on a deep relay in a long check-in blackout, counting cycles aloud to stay sure of time. That count is the anchor.
- Cass: a records clerk's child on an archive relay where logs rewrote themselves; remembered what the page said before.
- Eli: raised on a survey tender's sensor deck in the noise; found the one true return.
- Jonah: a tender whose comms ghosted familiar voices; learned to hear when a voice is not its speaker.
- Miri: an injuring exposure, and a body that preserved itself oddly (the markers the Panel wanted to open).
