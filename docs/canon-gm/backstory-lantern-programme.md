THE PROGRAMME (facts; the game shows them only as evidence)
- The Scout Service lost survey crews and relay records to "discontinuities", places where logs, instruments and people stop agreeing. [FROM RECORD: Origin, WorldHist]
- Dr. Adrian Vale noticed that Survey dependents who survived exposure as small children perceived the drift without being fooled. [FROM RECORD: Origin]
- Project Lantern (Special Projects) paired such children with partner minds, "Eidolons", to carry true records through a discontinuity. Security was Helena Voss. [FROM RECORD: Origin]
- An oversight panel (Scout plus naval intelligence), liaison Merrow, saw strategic value and moved the children through personnel logistics as "crew transfers." [FROM RECORD: Origin, WorldHist]
- Six children, six partner minds, six table roles: custodian Marcus, anchor (now Tally), archivist Cass, listener Eli, witness of voices Jonah, keeper Miri. [FROM RECORD: Origin]
- Numbers: Marcus One, Tally Two, Cass Three, Eli Four, Jonah Five, Miri Six; the seats of the count, see docs/canon-gm/lantern-numbers.md. [RULED 2026-10-20: PL5]
- Ages now: Marcus about 30; Miri 15; the rest adult.

THE ESCAPE (one event, about 19 years before Day 0)
- At Halworth a group exercise failed over 41 hours. The panel reclassified the children as custodial assets and set a procedure against Six. [FROM RECORD: Origin, WorldHist]
- The partner mind EID-0 refused, with Marcus. He divided route knowledge among the six and asked for removals. The children scattered. Miri went into protected suspension. [FROM RECORD: Origin, Ledger]
- The anchor was sealed in stasis at Halworth. Ilse Vantor reported the gap and was reassigned under Merrow's countersignature. [FROM RECORD: Origin, Halworth]

AFTER
- The panel was dissolved, but a clause "continued" custody of its wards, directors and assets under one obscure office. [FROM RECORD: WorldHist]
- Heirs: a state cell, a clinic (Hesperis), a logistics house (Serein), and a contractor estate. See who-pursues. [FROM RECORD: Origin]
- Helena burned the cell's watch and vanished about eleven years ago. Whereabouts HELD. [FROM RECORD: Origin]
