# Theme
- Space opera with moral cost: exploration, mystery, dark shades; choices matter and doing right costs. [FROM RECORD: Plan §0]
- Spine, never said aloud: who holds the dark safe, by consent or by custody, and what it costs to choose consent. Every offered approach sits on one side. [FROM RECORD: Plan §1; Idx]
- Echoes, played and not explained: the wolf's "safe passage" and the head's "a Hunter that guards the lanes" are one pitch; the debrief that took Marcus's memory "to protect him" and the clause that "continues" Adrian are one custody; forged-down debt tallies answer a ward list; Lantern's mesh and the Lighthouse nodes are one network shape. [FROM RECORD: Plan §1; PU]
- Every episode's spine is a voyage, run, pursuit or survey; paper work is a 3-5 line report and one choice. [FROM RECORD: Plan §0]

# People and tone
- Renata is not a villain. The wolf's captain is courteous, never shouts, never explains the tool. The Section's head is a believer with a case worth respecting. Merrow can be useful, frightened and honest, never redeemed. [FROM RECORD: Plan §2-3, §5]
- The "shoal talking" and the nature of the discontinuities stay unexplained all season. [FROM RECORD: Plan §3A.4, §3C]
- A named ally's death must be seeded two scenes earlier, tied to a refused cost, avoidable at a price, and given a full scene. [FROM RECORD: Plan §10 rulings]
- Miri (15, protected) is never aboard into danger and never a source of answers. Tally (they/them) is a pilot trainee, never an answer key, and picks one small thing for themself each beat. [FROM RECORD: Plan §0, §6]

# Pacing lessons
- Players found one Season 3 episode slow and dry because of money tables; now one cycle card per turn, and no money scene unless a cash choice is live. [FROM RECORD: Eco]
- Over-surfacing legal status made play feel like compliance; keep procedure backstage unless it creates danger, cost or choice. [FROM RECORD: Mech]
- Recurring characters sounded too professional; dramatise limits instead of naming them. [FROM RECORD: Mech]
- One sitting ran well past five scenes; cut harder. [FROM RECORD: S3H]
- Arc 4's finale must turn on a person in a room, not a broadcast. [FROM RECORD: Plan §4.4]
