Name: *Ardent Venture*
Class and size: 600-ton merchant liner hull; jump 2, thrust 1 (ref). Six hardpoints, all used, none pop-up (ref).
Role: the fleet's heavy hauler and carrier; lab, sickbay and a medium base ride in her. Named as the hull on station at the traffic hab for a cycle.
Quirks: slow in a system and slow off a berth; no hangar, so small craft ride in the cargo bay and are lifted in and out at rest; "huge and plain, like a house that has come to the country"; Faraday-backed option, alliance-crewed.
What upkeep and fuel feel like: charter Cr12,000 and maintenance about Cr11,900 a cycle (ref); fuel for about 24 weeks, scoops and processors (ref). On station she earns the light rate, not the heavy run, a difference of about Cr42,000 a cycle.
Who flies her: Maret, master of record; Jae, pilot and astrogator; Iria and Tomas, engineers; Milos, sensors; Niko, steward and medic; Dob and Tobin on the guns.
Sickbay, workshop, launch bay: sickbay, laboratory, workshop and armoury (ref). The cargo bay carries a shuttle and a launch; it is not a launch bay.
Three details only someone who has flown her would know:
1. In jump, Iria keeps a hand flat on the drive casing.
2. For jump she "dims", halving her basic power; she cannot jump from a fight with her manoeuvre drive running (ref).
3. Her wardroom is wide and plain, one long table, a great many chairs, nearly empty.
