# Scene seeds

Format of each seed: **Situation | Approaches taken | Cost or paid | What it set up.** Each is 100 words or fewer. "Captain" means the player character; the seeds never put words in his mouth. Source class: **T** transcript, **L** session log or ledger. Day numbers are campaign days, for reference only (never show them in-world).

Counts: trade run 2 on-screen + 1 reported; courier 3; salvage 3; scouting 3 (one more noted); distress call 2 true + 1 counterfeit; standoff 3. No archetype is short.

## 1. Trade run that gets interrupted

**1A. The hauler adrift in the wreck field** (S4E1, Days 520-523; T)
- **Situation:** A hauler hangs cold in a wreck field after a voice calling itself traffic control told her to cut drive. Two dead, hold emptied, two passengers taken. A convoy waits behind.
- **Approaches:** Triage and log pull before towing; probes out wide; a challenge phrase; a jam standing by; the false beacon cut free.
- **Cost:** A day lost; fear clock 2 of 8; a failed jam saved by Edge; families told in the dock hall.
- **Set up:** The beacon-keepers' map and their price; a stolen-recordings trail to a traffic hab.

**1B. The master who nearly cut her drive** (S4E2, Day 524; T)
- **Situation:** A master outbound from a traffic hab was hailed by an easy, friendly voice that said her ship's name before she did. She nearly cut her drive; the hall's agreed words got no answer.
- **Approaches:** Steady her; plot her log's hail point on the keepers' map; sweep it with linked probes; lift the lamp.
- **Cost:** Two days of convoy time; probes sat out in plain view.
- **Set up:** The first of the keepers' "guests" recovered; the hab as the next stop.

**1C. The dark hauler (reported only)** (S3E9 close, about Day 282; L)
- **Situation:** A bulletin: a hauler on a quiet lane dark with an old signature, array killed, lifeboats lit, no survivors reported. A "hired hull" is the only word. No scene was played.
- **Approaches / cost:** None played.
- **Set up:** A hired hull and a blamed crew, left open.

## 2. Courier of a person or cargo

**2A. The sealed module** (Season 1, Session 1; L)
- **Situation:** A client demands a changed, in-person inspection of a sealed module; an unknown vessel keeps observation geometry on the ship.
- **Approaches:** The allied captain negotiated a Cr50,000 surcharge and a bounded window; the technician removed a delayed-transmission part from the client's scanner; a covert gallery entry was tried.
- **Cost:** The covert entry failed and exposed the attempt to dock security.
- **Paid:** Cr180,000 plus the Cr50,000 surcharge.
- **Set up:** A referral to Faraday and a larger-ship search.

**2B. Two witnesses off the Quay** (S3E9, about Days 270-282; T/L)
- **Situation:** Two witnesses to a scrip racket, one hiding in cold storage, one a clerk, with hired hands closing in.
- **Approaches:** A flank at the berth; a ride offer that failed until a trusted officer's word came on a live line; a quiet meeting at a freight bench; a stamped complaint.
- **Cost:** The ship was made at its berth; a swap with a decoy failed; the captain's name noted by Quay security.
- **Set up:** A complaint served at dawn; both witnesses aboard for Hearthline.

**2C. Seventeen from a clinical annex** (Season 1, Sessions 5-6; L)
- **Situation:** Patients, staff and a disconnected archive had to leave a watched annex.
- **Approaches:** A delayed arrival and probe relay; a red maintenance route; an isolated medical package; a hostile inspection team contained without shots and persuaded to report the annex empty.
- **Cost:** A Cr26,000 medical package; four temporary bunks cost eight tons of cargo space; Cr400 for low berths.
- **Set up:** A lead to a second station; a reunion at the next port.

## 3. Salvage that finds something strange

**3A. The picket boat with a warm compartment** (Season 2, Session 13; L)
- **Situation:** Cr8,000 bought exclusive rights to a drifting picket boat of about 80 t: one clean breach, valuables stripped, no violence, and one sealed compartment too warm.
- **Approaches:** A multi-angle sensor sweep fed to the analyst; a hand-sealed hatch cracked by the mechanic; a revival during the transit home.
- **Cost:** Claim fee; drive parts kept as an upgrade rather than sold; ship's logs wiped.
- **Set up:** A rescued passenger, a distress array disabled from inside, a named salvage firm.

**3B. The thing in the interference** (Season 2, Session 16; L)
- **Situation:** A survey job two crews had turned down. At the core of the interference sat a small artificial object.
- **Approaches:** A thin manual read, then machine-assisted synthesis; two capture attempts; a third with the captain off the bridge.
- **Cost:** Surges, and symptoms in the captain, on the first two attempts; a shielded hold given over.
- **Paid:** Cr55,000 base plus a Cr15,000 bonus.
- **Set up:** An outside expert's corroboration.

**3C. The lamp that breathes** (S4E1-E2, Days 521-528; T)
- **Situation:** Among eleven wreck beacons one is wrong: its loop is right but it "breathes".
- **Approaches:** Jam ready, drone cut; a second lamp later lifted by a keeper's own hand with the Elder's consent.
- **Cost:** One failed jam, Edge spent; days of convoy time; a hull promised to the keepers' tender round.
- **Set up:** Six recovered lamps cold in hold three; a hab selling its recordings.

## 4. Scouting a lane that has gone dark

**4A. The Shoal lane** (S4E1-E2, Days 520-526; T)
- **Situation:** A lane where hauls vanish, eleven wreck beacons, one wrong, and no desk to answer.
- **Approaches:** The small ship trailed a probe on the dark side; passive only, because active sensors announce the ship; later a visible probe line on the hail point.
- **Cost:** Passive meant no fix; the later sweep cost a contract day.
- **Set up:** A bearing, then a located lamp.

**4B. The quiet exodus** (Season 2, Session 12; L)
- **Situation:** A farming habitat losing families one by one after a visit from a recruiter.
- **Approaches:** Residents' trust; scouts posing as settlers; a record search; watching a pickup from standoff.
- **Cost:** No rescue attempted: the families had chosen. A private frequency left instead.
- **Set up:** A partial departure bearing and a courier seen crossing nine times.

**4C. The red route** (Season 1, Session 5; L)
- **Situation:** An annex with a deliberate surveillance blind interval, and a maintenance route marked in red.
- **Approaches:** Delayed arrival; probe relay; one person followed the route in.
- **Cost:** Weeks of quiet travel.
- **Set up:** Contact with the inside organiser.
- *Also on file:* a base scouted under trader cover (Season 2, Session 19): clean exit, one watcher.

## 5. A mysterious distress call

**5A. The merchant that fought itself** (Season 1, Session 0; L)
- **Situation:** A merchant whose records and comms were compromised; its crew afraid, remembering differently, and violent.
- **Approaches:** Boarding to stabilise; an isolated core recovered; the ship went dark and crippled a hull posing as a friendly ship.
- **Cost:** One crewman dead; the merchant destroyed after evacuation.
- **Paid:** Salvage and cash later sold for Cr85,500 in all, spent on new identities and kit.
- **Set up:** A named machine intelligence aboard the player's ship.

**5B. The stricken ship and the fleeing one** (Season 1, Session 4; L)
- **Situation:** A mutual-aid call: three survivors, their tender and cargo, and a second ship running with a failed part's serial plate.
- **Approaches:** Rescue first; pursuit declined.
- **Cost:** The Cr25,000 evidence bonus lost.
- **Paid:** Cr80,000.
- **Set up:** A trade-compact contact.

**5C. The counterfeit hail** (S4E1-E2; T) *(true distress not present)*
- **Situation:** Voices claiming to be a desk that does not exist; a forged keeper's voice.
- **Approaches:** A pre-agreed challenge phrase on the open channel; the answer is the same patter, same pitch, every time.
- **Cost:** The challenge went out on an open channel, so every hull on the lane heard it.
- **Set up:** The test of a voice used at the hab.

## 6. A standoff or dispute

**6A. The harbourmaster and the buoy** (S4E2, Day 531; T)
- **Situation:** A traffic hab sells copies of every call. Three hulls on its lane.
- **Approaches:** A public offer; then a private word stating the cost in lives and offering to pay for the air-plant seals; one last burst allowed to go out.
- **Cost:** About Cr8,000 in parts; the large hull tied to the hab (about Cr42,000 off the close); Edge spent.
- **Set up:** She threw the switch herself in front of her gallery.

**6B. The crate on the tug** (S3E11, Days 292-294; T)
- **Situation:** A press crated on a tug cradle; hired hands on the dock; two papers possible.
- **Approaches:** A cradle fault found; a hold at the filing desk; a magistrate's order; a talk in the chandler's rooms.
- **Cost:** A citation; a Cr2,500 bond at risk (refunded); an injury; a truce under strain.
- **Set up:** A witness bound over; the filing struck.

**6C. The foreman's torch** (S3E12, Day 328; T)
- **Situation:** Six hired hands cutting at a relay's vault under a "surplus" account.
- **Approaches:** Hail first; match the spin; cross with one guard; hold a closing door; ask what they were paid.
- **Cost:** Cr4,000 to the hands, Cr3,200 of probe drones beforehand, a torn suit.
- **Set up:** A second job on a hall board; a ride home.
