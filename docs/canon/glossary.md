Forty terms at most. Format: term, meaning, rule for use.

People and forms of address
1. Captain: how strangers and officials address Marcus ("Captain Torin" from Maret, "Captain" from Merrow). Renata says "Torin"; friends say "Marcus". The game never writes Marcus's speech.
2. Lantern: a title for the survivors who carry the old abilities (Lantern One, Three, Four). Never an organisation, ship or crew. In the game it appears only as evidence, for example a stranger who uses the title.
3. Seneschal: Priya's title in the alliance. Never "Seneschal of Hearthline".
4. Ward: a person held under the Compact's authority (Merrow).
5. Protected: someone under the alliance's promise, not crew.
6. Harbourmaster: head of the traffic hab. "Keeper" is reserved for the beacon-keepers.

Groups and places
7. Hearthline (the ring): the ring station and its volume; home port. People are at it, not of it.
8. The Compact: the Hearthline Compact, the authority (Halvorsen for the board, Renata, Costa). Never the station.
9. The alliance: Marcus's group of crews, lower case. "Lighthouse" appears only inside "Lighthouse Council".
10. The fleet: the four hulls (Errant Pawn, Ardent Venture, Black Sun Rising, Beacon). The patrol cutter is the Compact's.
11. The volume: space within Jump-1 of Hearthline; work beyond it pays more.
12. Hab: a small habitat station.
13. The Shoal, the Corvid run: the salvage belt and its escort lane. Not to be confused with Thessen's "Corvid's ..." ship names.
14. The Psalter: the beacon-keepers of the wreck field; keepers, tender, tending.
15. The Section, the Coordination Office: Scout-era authority. The Section signed Merrow's claim; the Office keeps old paper in force. Neither is a place.

Paper and law
16. Register: the arms register, every arm on every hull, filed each cycle. A count, not a limit.
17. Self-defence clause: the written right to defend life, ship and traffic under attack. The price is review.
18. After-action review: filed and reviewed after any use of force.
19. Sealed carry: a sidearm worn sealed and visible on the ring.
20. Charter: the Compact's written terms. Clause (b), "Coordination Office instruments in force", is undefined.
21. Continuance authority: a clause nobody will define (TBD). Quote it; do not attribute it.
22. Segment designation: the number in Marcus's folder; a segment of something with no chart.

Time and money
23. Cycle: 28 days; the books close on it. A week is 7 days, a leg 14, a season 84.
24. Day N, watch: days count from Day 0 (the jump to Faraday Basin). Written "Day 514, second watch". Watch length: (TBD).
25. Cr: Credits, written "Cr159,415", no space.
26. Jump-1, Jump-2: jump rating, hyphenated. Faraday Basin is Jump-1 from Hearthline.
27. Light rate, hull tied down: a hab pays a hull's light rate while she sits on its station instead of working.

Lane talk
28. Lane, marker: a route and its waypoints; "the third marker" is where the keepers meet convoys.
29. Lamp, kept lamp: a wreck beacon. A kept lamp loops a captain's last words and does not listen.
30. Guest: a false lamp that listens and forwards. The keepers say "a guest that answers is a guest".
31. Page challenge: a question only real people can answer, sent on the open channel to test a voice.
32. Agreed words: the phrase a hall agrees for confirming a hail.
33. "Thanks for your patience": the forged traffic voice's tell, the same words at the same pitch every time.
34. Jam: drowning a carrier. Probe, picket: station-keeping scouts strung out as a watch line.

Naming patterns
35. Ship names: two plain words, often a virtue or a trade word (Honest Thistle, Faithful Compass, Small Reckoning, Lantern Fish, Cold Wake, Second Wind). Thessen hulls take "Corvid's ..." (Corvid's Debt). Italic in prose.
36. Place names: a name plus a landform or trade word (Corda's Reach, Halven's Rest, Corrin's Well, Renner's Hollow, Mercy Span, Anvil Quay, Marrow Deep, Larkspur Drift, Fenrir Gap).
37. People's names: given names carry most weight. Surnames are mixed (Castellan, Nkema, Vantor, Sandvik, Petrakis, Okonjo). The shared surname Vale is used with a given name only. Tally is they/them.
