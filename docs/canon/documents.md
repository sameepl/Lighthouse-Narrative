# In-world documents

## House style (what the files show)

- **Header line in capitals** naming the issuing body and the form: "HEARTHLINE COMPACT", "ANVIL QUAY FILING DESK", "ARMS REGISTER".
- **Short labelled lines** (Label: value), one fact each; numbered terms for charters; a stamp, seal or clerk's initial at the foot ("Received and held", "Stamped, [clerk]"). Papers are squared on the desk and come in ones, twos and threes of pages: "one page, three lines".
- **Tone:** flat clerk's hand. No adjectives. Sums as Cr2,500. Ship names in italics. Places by ring, spur, bay, cradle or lane number ("bay 31", "cradle 2", "Ring D", "lane two", "Suite 14-C").
- **Time** is a watch (first, second, third) and a cycle, never a day number. Core 04: nobody says, in speech or in a document, "a skill level or 'trained', a roll, DM or Effect, Edge, a standing tier, a Day number, 'booked', an advance, or a season or episode number." Plain durations, job words, prices and in-world odds are fine.
- **Redaction** is shown as "[removed]" or "REDACTED", never blank.

## 1. Charter page

*Seen:* S4E0 (the Compact officer's charter, "close-set, with the Compact seal at the head"; "hull by hull, for berth, standing and arms authority, with hours"). Sample is a composite; the second obligation (a recognition clause the alliance struck on its counter) is left out on purpose.

```text
HEARTHLINE COMPACT
CHARTER (PROVISIONAL RATING)            [Compact seal]

1. Party: the alliance, by named hull.
2. Per hull: berth, standing, arms authority,
   hours (Schedule A).
3. Alliance hulls on Compact lanes accept
   Compact traffic control.
4. Offer stands one full cycle.
   Nothing binds until signed.

Received and held. [clerk's initials]
```

## 6. Log fragment

*Seen:* S4E1 (a hauler's galley logger: "the hail, the shout, the same reply after the shout"); S4E1-E2 (the crew's own comms log, "logged at the minute"); S3E12 (a relay's housekeeping log that repeats every event twice, an hour apart).

```text
GALLEY LOGGER - second watch, two days out
[hail] "Cut the drive for inspection.
        Thanks for your patience."
[crew, raised voice]
[hail] "Cut the drive for inspection.
        Thanks for your patience."
[outer lock: cycled. no response]
```
*Verbatim:* "Thanks for your patience" is the false voice's line in S4E1; the repeat in the same pitch is the tell.
