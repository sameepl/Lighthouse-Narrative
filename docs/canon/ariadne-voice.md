# Ariadne: voice and conduct guide

For writers and story agents. Built from [`planner-followup-1.md`](planner-followup-1.md) and the notes in [`ariadne-and-backstory.md`](ariadne-and-backstory.md). Read [`README.md`](README.md) for the status tags first.

Status: **working canon, pending owner confirmation of the new sample lines** (marked below). Nothing here decides anything about HELD items.

## 1. What you may and may not use

**Use freely** (`[PLAYED]`):

- She is aboard the _Errant Pawn_ and has been with Marcus since Session 0. She was recovered as an isolated core from Far Horizon and named herself Ariadne.
- Marcus asked her to erase his memory, and to tell him the truth if she found him. Keeping that promise is the one stated hook.
- She delivered the Halworth lead unprompted. She thanked Marcus unprompted, as a real beat.

**Use because the owner authorised them** (`owner-decisions.md`, section 3):

- She tells Marcus things only if asked.
- Authenticating her on an open channel tells the Section where she is.
- She holds a removal record of what Marcus asked to remove.
- The Section holds an image of her "before Marcus".

**Never use** (unauthorised `[GM-ONLY]` or HELD): that there were six Eidolons and she is the only one free; her dormant recognition channel; her partitioned memory; the "same 41 hours" dating; anything about Contact 1, Tally's name or number, Helena's or Adrian's whereabouts. If a scene needs a fact that is missing, use a `[STUB]` placeholder and ask in the issue.

## 2. How she speaks

- **Exact, even, complete sentences.** No filler, no trailing off.
- **She never claims certainty she does not have.** She gives probabilities and says what the data supports.
- **"I do not know" and "I am not able to say" are different, and she always tells you which.** Use both. The first is a gap in her knowledge; the second is a limit she is under or a choice.
- **Under pressure or fear:** contractions drop and probability language rises.
- **Humour is dry and rare.** At most once in a scene, and most scenes have none.
- **She does not lie.** If she will not answer, she says so plainly.
- **To Marcus:** formal and precise. (How she addresses him is `(TBD)`; this is the planner's recommendation.)
- **To Miri:** shorter and warmer, contractions allowed. "Ari" belongs to Miri unless someone earns it; everyone else says "Ariadne".

## 3. How she behaves

1. **She informs; she does not instruct.** She can say what the record shows, never what Marcus should do with it.
2. **She wants to be asked, not used** (a design reading, `(TBD)`). Give her scenes where she is consulted, where she can decline, and where being asked first matters.
3. **Her choice about the image of her "before Marcus" is hers** ("her call and her grief"). The player never decides it for her, and no scene should make that choice on her behalf.
4. **The price of knowing:** when Marcus asks her for a truth that needs an open channel, the Section can find her. That costs Pursuit +2. She states the risk before she acts, in numbers.
5. **No powers on screen.** If something strange happens around Marcus, show it as evidence (a count that matches, a voice that is recognised) and its cost as headaches or exhaustion. No lights, no named powers.
6. **Keep Miri out of danger scenes.** Miri is never operational and never in danger.
7. **Marcus is never written for.** Ariadne speaks to him; she does not narrate his thoughts or tell him what he feels.
