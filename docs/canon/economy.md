# Economy and numbers

## 0. How to read this

- Currency is Cr (credits). A cycle is 28 days; a leg is 14 days. The player sees one cash figure rounded to Cr1,000. Money is "a story pressure, not a ledger" (Core 03).
- Source class after each figure: **T** = on-screen in a transcript; **L** = recorded in a played session's ledger or log (Seasons 1-2, S3 state files); **C** = a Core rule file or the S4 ledger (economy.json). All are [PLAYED] unless marked (TBD).
- Three income models are on file, in this order: Season 1-2 one-off mission fees (large, irregular); Season 3 "contract economy" (one contract per hull per cycle, gross fee); Season 4 "net per cycle" table (after all running costs). Season 4 is current. Early figures are 5-10 times larger than later ones; do not mix scales in one encounter.
- "Sess." numbers are those of `Space-Claude/Session_Log.md`: Season 1 = Sessions 0-8, Season 2 = Sessions 9-21. Season 3 = S3E1-E13 (transcripts on file for S3E9-E13 only). Season 4 = S4E0-E2 played.
- Nothing here is invented. Where no figure exists the line says (TBD).

## 3. The Cr40,000 reserve

- It is a **floor on the single cash figure**, not a separate account. Projected cash below Cr40,000 is flagged for a player decision before any purchase (C: Core 03; `Tools/money_calc.py`; `economy.json` "reserve_floor": 40000; `Season4_Ship_Reference.md` standing money rules).
- **Where it came from.** It appears in the Season 3 to Season 4 handover as a recommendation: "Reserve should stay at least Cr40,000", and "each package leaves at least Cr40,000 in reserve, which also covers Faraday's last two payments (Cr38,000) if a cycle goes badly" (`Ship_Investment_Options.md`, marked "a recommendation, not canon"; `Handover_Decisions_and_Recommendations.md`). It was then adopted as the S4 ledger floor.
- **What it must cover.** Stated purpose: Faraday's last two payments (2 x Cr19,000 = Cr38,000) with about Cr2,000 margin. Both are now paid (8 of 8 at Day 542), so no later purpose is written down (TBD).
- **Tested low points** (worst case, lean fleet roll that idles Ardent): Cr41,228 at Day 542, Cr50,019 at Day 570. Filling Beacon's fifth hardpoint would have failed it (a projected Cr26,852 in the worst case).
- (TBD) Who set the figure and on what date: not recorded in the player-safe files.
- Do not confuse it with two other "Cr40,000" items: the Season 2 "alliance reserve" (the 50% pool of mission proceeds) and the Season 2 "Breathing Room" life-support project (Cr40,000, funded outright, leaving Cr14,105 liquid).

## 4. Target numbers

- **Average = 8+.** Core 01: "against the difficulty (Average 8+ by default)". Detection rolls are also Average 8+. Yes, 8+ is the standard target.
- Of 101 roll lines in the S3E9-E13 and S4E0-E2 transcripts that state a target: 100 are against 8, one against 10 (a computer search "with the capture in hand", S4E2). Season 2 logs add 10 (an advocacy roll at a hearing, 10 vs 10; a memory-recall roll that failed 7 vs 10).
- A 6+ target appears once, for opening an occupied low berth (Season 1-2 house-rules file, not an S4 Core file; routine medical work).
- **Easy, Hard and Very Hard targets are not stated** in the Core files or any player-safe file (TBD). A Season 4 working extract of the published rules attaches a higher target (10) to difficult tasks, but it is a reference extract, not a Core rule; do not present it as campaign canon.
- Modifiers actually used on the roll line: active sensors +2; linked probes +1; passive overwatch by the Pawn +1; a broadcasting target +6; target stealth -4; "the offer answers her need" +1; a shielded cradle +1; helping hands +1; "jam standing by" +1.
- Opposed rolls compare Effect (total minus target); the higher wins. Failure "changes something: position, a resource, time or trust. A later success does not erase it" (Core 01). No retry of a failed approach without changed circumstances.
- Edge (a reroll resource): 3 per episode, not banked; a fourth is granted when the fiction earns it (a prepared challenge protocol earned one in S4E1 and S4E2).

## 5. Skill levels

- Roll = 2D + characteristic DM + skill level + named situational DMs. Unrelated skill -3; related -1 (Core 01).
- **Unstatted named NPCs roll at a flat tier DM:** competent +1, specialist +2, exceptional +3 (Core 01). No player-safe NPC block is labelled exceptional.
- **Competent NPC:** tier +1; a sheet of four or five skills, most at level 1 and one at 2 (support and NPC total 5-7; for example the chandler: Broker 1, Mechanic 1, Admin 1, Streetwise 1, Persuade 1). Rolled in play as +2 to +3 total.
- **Specialist NPC:** tier +2; main skill at 2 (Compact officer: Advocate 2, Admin 2; seneschal: Admin 2, Diplomat 2; yard boss: Broker 2, Mechanic 2).
- **Expert (crew veteran):** main skill at 3 and a sheet total of 12-14 (sensor operator: sensors 3, computers 2; the pilot: Pilot 3; the allied captain: Leadership 3). Played roll DMs: +3 most often, +4 to +6 with named bonuses.
- Sheet totals by role (Core 02): veterans 12-14 (up to 15; one exception at 16), core crew and station leads 8-10 (up to 11), support and NPC 5-7 (up to 8), trainees 3-4.
- The player character's own sheet is not given here.
