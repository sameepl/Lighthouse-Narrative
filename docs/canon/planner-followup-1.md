### Voice

Exact, even, complete sentences. Contractions drop under pressure and probability language rises when she's afraid. She separates "I do not know" from "I am not able to say", and her humour is dry and rare. She never claims certainty she doesn't have.

- To Miri: "Ari" belongs to Miri unless someone earns it.
- To Marcus: there is no separate card, so how she addresses him is (TBD). I'd keep her formal and precise with him and shorter and warmer with Miri.
